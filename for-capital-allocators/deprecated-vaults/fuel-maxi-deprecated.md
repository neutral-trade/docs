## How does it work:

<figure><img src="https://1924233991-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F6YjCIQB3ardoMeqGMIF8%2Fuploads%2FDXzdtCIuuPuglBi3vVxb%2Fchrome_rZpomS4zKa.png?alt=media&#x26;token=5308a180-62bc-47f6-bb82-1539ea7073e8" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

AUSD is collateralized on Drift, earning lending yield. SOL is then borrowed and swapped into dSOL, earning a 5x FUEL multiplier too while dSOL staking yield and AUSD lending yield cover the SOL borrowing.
