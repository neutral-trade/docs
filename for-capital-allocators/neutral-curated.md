# Neutral Curated

Neutral Curated is the main lineup on Neutral Trade. Every strategy in it passed our full due diligence and a period of live testing with Neutral capital before it opened for deposits. It is the default view on the [strategies page](https://www.neutral.trade/strategies).

## What Is in Neutral Curated

| Category                                     | What it is                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Neutral Autopilot](neutral-autopilot.md)    | A strategy of strategies: one deposit, allocated across curated strategies.   |
| [Market Neutral](usdc-yield/market-neutral/) | Strategies that aim to earn from market structure, not from market direction. |
| [Directional](usdc-yield/directional/)       | Strategies that take long or short exposure to capture trends.                |
| [RWA](usdc-yield/rwa/)                       | Strategies that trade real-world assets, such as equities.                    |
| [SOL Yield](sol-yield/)                      | Strategies that accept SOL deposits.                                          |

[Meridian Liquidity Provider](usdc-yield/market-neutral/meridian-liquidity-provider.md) is listed in [Neutral Marketplace](neutral-marketplace.md), not in Neutral Curated.

## How a Strategy Becomes Curated

A trading firm goes through the full process in [Curator Selection](../curators/curator-selection.md) and [Curator Onboarding](../curators/curator-onboarding.md). In short:

* **Full KYB** on the firm.
* **Full due diligence** on the strategy, the risk management, and the track record.
* **Live testing with Neutral capital** before the strategy opens for deposits.
* **Neutral Trade vault infrastructure.** The firm receives trading authority only. It cannot withdraw your deposit. See [Vault Protections](../neutral-strategy-vaults/vault-protections.md).

Only Neutral Curated strategies are eligible for an allocation from Neutral Autopilot.

## Neutral Curated and Neutral Marketplace

| Check                                                | Neutral Curated | [Neutral Marketplace](neutral-marketplace.md) |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Full KYB on the firm                                 | Yes             | Yes                                           |
| Runs on Neutral Trade accounts, firm cannot withdraw | Yes             | Yes                                           |
| Track record reviewed                                | Full            | Basic                                         |
| Strategy and risk due diligence                      | Full            | Basic                                         |
| Live testing with Neutral capital                    | Yes             | Not yet                                       |
| Allocation from Neutral Autopilot                    | Eligible        | Not allocated                                 |

Due diligence reduces risk. It does not remove it. A curated strategy can still lose money. Read the [Risk Overview](risk-overview/) before you deposit.
