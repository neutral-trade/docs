# USDC Yield

USDC Yield strategies take USDC deposits on Solana. Your position is measured in USDC, so returns and losses are in dollar terms. Choose a category by how much market exposure, liquidity, and variance you want.

Want one deposit across several strategies? [Neutral Autopilot](../neutral-autopilot.md) allocates USDC across multiple underlying strategies for you.

Want to grow your SOL instead of USDC? See [SOL Yield](../sol-yield/).

## Choosing a Category

| Category       | Market exposure                    | Typical redemptions    | Variance |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------- |
| Market Neutral | Hedged, low net exposure           | Weekly or twice weekly | Lower    |
| Directional    | Long and short exposure on purpose | Weekly                 | Higher   |
| RWA            | Traditional markets                | Quarterly, with notice | Varies   |
| NT Earn        | None. Lends USDC                   | Normally within 1 hour | Lowest   |

Exact terms for each vault are on its strategy page and in the vault's Details view in the app. See [Fees + Redemption Period](../../additional-info/fees-+-redemption-period.md) and the [Risk Overview](../risk-overview/).
