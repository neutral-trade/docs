---
description: >-
  Real World Assets (RWA) are traditional financial items - such as real estate,
  government bonds, gold, or commodities that are represented as digital tokens
  on a blockchain.
---

# RWA

To expand yield sources from pure crypto trading, RWA allows offchain investment products, such as Equity Funds, to be listed onchain through a third party tokenization platform.&#x20;
