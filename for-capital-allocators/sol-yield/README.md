# SOL Yield

SOL Yield vaults are for holders who want to keep their SOL and grow it. You deposit SOL. The vault runs a professional trading strategy, and its returns are used to buy more SOL. Your position is measured in SOL, so the goal is a larger SOL balance over time.

All strategies in this category are run by external curator teams that have passed Neutral Trade's [Curator Selection](../../curators/curator-selection.md) process.

## What to Know Before You Allocate

* **You keep SOL price exposure.** Your deposit stays in SOL. The vault aims to grow the number of SOL you hold, but the USD value of your position still moves with the SOL price.
* **Returns are not guaranteed.** The underlying strategy can lose money. A loss reduces the number of SOL in the vault.
* **Fees and redemptions** are shown on each strategy page and in the vault's Details view in the app. See [Fees + Redemption Period](../../additional-info/fees-+-redemption-period.md).
