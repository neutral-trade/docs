# Directional

Directional strategies take market exposure on purpose. Quantitative signals decide when a strategy is in or out of the market. Returns move with their markets to some degree, so expect higher variance and a wider range of monthly results than in market-neutral strategies.

The strategies in this category are listed on [Neutral Marketplace](../). They passed basic due diligence: we met the team, we understand the strategy, and we completed full KYB on the firm. Neutral Trade has not yet tested them with its own capital, and [Neutral Autopilot](../../neutral-autopilot.md) does not allocate to them.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**A Marketplace listing is not an endorsement of performance.** A newer strategy has a shorter record, so its results are less certain. Deposit only what you can afford to lose.
{% endhint %}

## Key Risks

* **Market risk.** These strategies carry market exposure. A move against the position causes a loss.
* **Drawdowns.** Drawdowns are an expected part of the return profile. Review each strategy's drawdown and volatility before you allocate.
* **Short record.** Marketplace strategies can rely on a short live record or on simulated results. Read each strategy page to see which applies.
* **Leverage and liquidation.** Leverage amplifies gains and losses. A sharp adverse move can trigger forced liquidation.
* **Venue and counterparty risk.** An exchange or custodian can fail while it holds deployed capital.

Read [Market & Strategy Risk](../../risk-overview/market-and-strategy-risk.md) and [Venue, Counterparty & Curator Risk](../../risk-overview/venue-counterparty-and-curator-risk.md) for the full picture.
