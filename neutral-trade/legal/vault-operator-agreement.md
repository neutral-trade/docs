# Vault Operator Agreement

This Vault Operator Agreement (“Agreement”) is made on the Effective Date by and between the User and the Operator.

The User and the Operator are individually a “Party”, and collectively the “Parties”.

## 1. INTERPRETATION

**1.1.** Capitalised terms used in this Agreement have the meanings set out in Section 12.

**1.2.** In this Agreement: (i) the singular includes the plural and the plural includes the singular; (ii) words importing any gender include all genders, where the context requires; and (iii) the words “include,” “includes,” “including,” “in particular,” and similar expressions are illustrative only and do not limit the meaning of the words that come before them.

## 2. FORMATION

**2.1.** The User accepts this Agreement by ticking the acceptance box presented in the Vault dashboard before providing Accepted Resources to the Vault. This Agreement is entered into and becomes binding on the Parties on the date when the User first provides Accepted Resources to the Vault (“Effective Date”).

**2.2.** This Agreement governs the relationship between the User and the Operator in respect of the Vault to which the User provides Accepted Resources. Any later provision of Accepted Resources by the User to that same Vault is also governed by this Agreement.

**2.3.** If the User provides Accepted Resources to any other Vault operated by the Operator, the relationship in respect of that other Vault will be governed by a separate agreement or separate terms applicable to that Vault.

**2.4.** Notwithstanding Section 2.1, where the User holds a Vault Share on the date on which this Agreement is first made available through the Vault dashboard (the “Publication Date”), the User is deemed to have accepted this Agreement in respect of that Vault Share with effect from the Publication Date, and the Publication Date is the Effective Date for that User. With effect from the Publication Date: (i) the Accepted Resources represented by that Vault Share shall be treated as Accepted Resources provided by the User to the Vault under this Agreement; (ii) any right, title or interest of the User in or to those Accepted Resources passes to the Operator in accordance with Section 4.1; and (iii) the User’s rights in respect of that Vault Share shall be solely those set out in this Agreement and the Vault Parameters. The User may redeem that Vault Share at any time within thirty (30) days after the Publication Date in accordance with the Vault Parameters, and no Fees other than those that would otherwise apply to that redemption shall be charged.

## 4. USER ACCESS TO THE VAULT

**4.1.** To access the Vault, the User shall provide Accepted Resources. Accepted Resources are presented in the Vault dashboard. When the User provides Accepted Resources to the Vault, full legal and beneficial title to those Accepted Resources passes to the Operator. The User has no ownership or other proprietary right in any specific Accepted Resources and has only the contractual rights set out in this Agreement and the Vault Parameters, including the right to redeem its Vault Share. Subject to this Agreement and the Vault Parameters, the Operator may deal with the Accepted Resources in its discretion. In receiving and deploying Accepted Resources, the Operator acts as principal and not as the User’s agent, trustee, custodian or investment manager. The User’s entitlement is against the Operator under this Agreement and is not an entitlement to a portfolio managed on the User’s behalf.

**4.2.** By providing the Accepted Resources, the User acknowledges and agrees that the Accepted Resources will be used by the Operator in execution of the Strategy. Execution of the Strategy may include the deployment, redeployment, exchange, staking, lending, bridging, hedging, or other use of Accepted Resources provided by the User. The Operator may lend or otherwise transfer Accepted Resources to third-party counterparties, including Affiliates, on terms under which title passes to the counterparty and the Operator receives contractual repayment or other payment rights. Such counterparties may use the transferred assets for their own-account trading. The User does not become the lender to, or a party to the Operator’s agreement with, any such counterparty.

**4.3.** The Operator may exit the Vault and the Strategy subject to the Vault Parameters. The Operator does not guarantee that any exit will happen immediately or at any particular value.

**4.4.** The Operator may suspend, restrict, unwind, or end the User’s access to the Vault and/or involvement in the Strategy where reasonably necessary for legal, regulatory, sanctions, security, fraud prevention, or risk management reasons. Any such action may include suspending, restricting, or delaying the User’s ability to redeem its Vault Share.

**4.5.** The User may exit its involvement with the Vault only by redeeming its Vault Share, in each case subject to any applicable redemption procedures, lock-up periods, cooldown periods, and/or other conditions. All relevant information will be presented in the Vault dashboard. The User has no right to the return of any specific Accepted Resources.

**4.6.** Termination of this Agreement or the User’s request to redeem its Vault Share does not itself guarantee immediate redemption if the Vault Parameters, Vault’s structure, underlying systems or protocols, and/or other technical reasons do not permit immediate redemption.

**4.7.** The Operator may, on written notice through the Vault dashboard, require the User to redeem all or part of its Vault Share, and may effect such redemption on the User’s behalf, where (i) the User is or becomes a Sanctioned Person, is located in a Sanctioned Country, or appears on any exclusion list maintained by the Operator; (ii) any representation or warranty given by the User is or becomes untrue in a material respect; (iii) the User fails to provide information reasonably requested under this Agreement within a reasonable period; (iv) the User’s continued participation would, in the Operator’s reasonable opinion, cause the Operator, the Vault, or other Users to breach Applicable Law or be exposed to material legal, regulatory, tax, or sanctions risk; (v) redemption is required by Applicable Law or by a competent authority; or (vi) the Operator, acting reasonably, determines that an extreme market event is occurring in relation to the Vault. Any compulsory redemption shall be effected at the value determined under this Agreement and shall be subject to the same redemption procedures, liquidity constraints, and technical limits as a voluntary redemption.

**4.8.** Any airdrop, fork, governance token, staking or protocol reward, incentive, rebate, or other incidental benefit received by the Operator in connection with the deployment or use of Accepted Resources in the execution of the Strategy shall accrue to the Operator, and shall be dealt with by the Operator in accordance with the Vault Parameters. Where the Vault Parameters provide that such benefits are to be applied for the account of the Vault, the Operator shall apply them accordingly. The Operator is under no obligation to claim, capture, or realise any such benefit, or to participate in any governance process, and shall have no liability for not doing so.

## 5. OPERATION OF THE VAULT

**5.1.** The Operator operates the Vault and determines how Accepted Resources acquired by it under Section 4.1 are deployed for its own account, subject to this Agreement and the Vault Parameters. The arrangements for such deployment are referred to as the Strategy and shall be described in the Vault dashboard.

**5.2.** Deployment may include lending, staking, exchange, bridging, hedging and other transactions, whether undertaken directly or through counterparties or service providers. The User does not appoint the Operator to manage a portfolio on the User’s behalf.

**5.3.** The Operator shall act in good faith and in a commercially reasonable manner, in line with the Strategy and any operational constraints known to the Operator. The Operator does not undertake any obligation to achieve any Strategy goal, continuously optimise the Strategy, preserve the User’s Accepted Resources, or guarantee the profitability of the Strategy or the preservation of value of the User’s Accepted Resources.

**5.4.** The User acknowledges and agrees that the Operator and its Affiliates may operate other vaults, accounts, funds, or strategies, including strategies that are similar to, different from, or opposite to the Strategy, and may hold or trade the same or related assets for their own account or for other users. The Operator and its Affiliates may allocate their own capital, personnel, capacity and other resources among their activities in their discretion, subject to this Agreement and Applicable Law, and are not required to offer the User any transaction or opportunity. The Operator may transact with its Affiliates and may engage Affiliates as service providers, in each case on terms the Operator reasonably considers to be on an arm’s length basis. Nothing in this Section requires the Operator to give the Vault priority over any other vault, account, fund, or strategy, and the Operator has no obligation to disclose the terms of any other arrangement.

**5.5.** The Operator may change, suspend, unwind, or end a Strategy if the Operator reasonably believes this is advisable because of (without limitation): protocol changes, risk events, legal issues, liquidity issues, technical failures (including any failure, defect, exploit, or unavailability of any third-party platform, infrastructure, or smart contract on which the Vault depends), or other adverse conditions or events.

**5.6.** The Operator may perform its services directly or through delegates, advisers, agents or Affiliates, to the extent permitted by Applicable Law.
