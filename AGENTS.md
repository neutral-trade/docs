## Company brain

Before starting work, read `~/brains/neutral/map.md`. If the local map is unavailable, read the [map in neutral-trade/cerebro](https://github.com/neutral-trade/cerebro/blob/main/map.md).

Fill in the Learned section on every PR. “None” is a full answer.
